P52 (frozen): (a) the cycle sum counts injective choice functions on the locus;
(b) the same sum with cyclic traces reproduces mu off it; (c) the three cyclic orders
on four blocks separate off the locus and coincide on it.
(a) On the commuting locus: signed cycle sum against the SDR count.
        family  subsets  up to size   agree
           C_4       15           4    True

  The reason is exact: summing sgn over the permutations with a given cycle partition
  gives the Moebius function of the partition lattice, and Moebius inversion over the
  collisions turns choice functions constant on blocks into injective ones.

(c) The three cyclic orders on four blocks: one number on the locus, three off it.
        family  4-subsets  max spread on locus  max spread off  separates
           C_4          1             0.00e+00          0.1119       True

       K_{3,3}        126             0.00e+00          0.2387       True
  On the locus the three orders give one value, the intersection number; off it they
  give three. That split is what m_4 sees and no set system can express.
  P52 HOLDS. The deformed polynomial is the SDR count of a family of SUBSPACES: the
  same signed permutation sum, with the intersection number |cap e| replaced by the
  cyclic trace tr(prod A). On the locus that is the classical inclusion-exclusion
  count of partial systems of distinct representatives, hence the matching number of
  the incidence graph; off it the intersection number acquires a cyclic order and
  splits into three at four blocks, which is where and why the rigidity ends.
