  P37 holds. The coefficients are mixed discriminants, on and off the commuting
  locus. Nonnegativity of the alternating signs is Alexandrov's inequality for
  positive semidefinite arguments, and the Newton ratios above one are the
  consequence of real-rootedness. Neither is new, and neither is claimed as new;
  what the identity retires is the question of what the coefficients count off the
  locus. On the locus they count systems of distinct representatives; off it they
  are the continuous extension of that count and count nothing.
