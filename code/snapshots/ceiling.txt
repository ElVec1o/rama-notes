  P54 HOLDS. The second-order row of a cyclic word of length above 2a vanishes
  identically, because a point of a tight family lies in exactly a blocks and the
  blocks along an arc all contain the same point. The bound is tight at a = 3. At
  a = 2 it is exactly four, so the four-block ceiling is proved there and needs no
  linear algebra. At a >= 3 the ceiling still holds numerically but by a different
  mechanism: the rows of length five and six are nonzero and lie in the span of the
  shorter ones, and that dependence is not explained here. It is not LOCAL either: the
  expansion is local at K_{3,3} and the cube and global at Fano, so no single local
  identity will do it.
