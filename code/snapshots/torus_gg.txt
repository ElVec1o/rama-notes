CLAIM B: how much of Conjecture 10 does 'x outside spec(G^ab)' settle?
The test has to be certified. det(xI - A_G(z)) vanishes on a CURVE in T^b, so a
grid minimum proves nothing by itself. But f(z) = det(xI - A_G(z)) is a
trigonometric polynomial of degree one in each z_e, so its 3^b coefficients are
recovered exactly by a DFT on a 3-point grid, they bound the gradient, and a grid
search plus that Lipschitz constant certifies a positive lower bound. A sign
change on the grid certifies the opposite, that x lies in spec(G^ab).
