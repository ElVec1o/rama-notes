  Where the fraction is zero the uniform certificate closes at that lambda.
  Where it is not, the certificate as stated does NOT close: a right vertex with few
  children is only safe because its child's ratio sits well below B, which a uniform
  interval cannot express.  That is the precise defect, and it is the remaining work.
