the certificate succeeds exactly when y1 > tau^2, which is the conjecture for
that graph. As the graph grows y1 decreases toward tau^2, so the margin the
certificate needs shrinks. A uniform bound on P_m strong enough to give the
conjecture would already BE the conjecture: the difficulty has not moved.
