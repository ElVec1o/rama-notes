  criterion; 'only C_3 and C_4' was the frozen guess and it is false at larger block

  the quadrics vanish identically and the tangent cone is the whole kernel.
  P43 HOLDS in all three parts. The span and the rank are combinatorial, both of the
  form n minus a component count, and 2-regularity at D is the statement that the two
  graphs have the same components. The answer to whether A6 follows from 2-regularity
  at EVERY cone direction is NO: cross basis directions satisfy Q = 0 exactly and have
  rank at most 1. What saves them is not the criterion but an explicit curve, that of
  the cross configuration, so A6 stands at every direction tested by one route or the
  other, with no single route covering all of them.
