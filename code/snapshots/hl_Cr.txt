PART 2.  ||W_S||^2 against Q_S at level r = 3
========================================================================================
For ODD r we need C_r = sum_S (Q_S - ||W_S||^2) SMALL, so we want ||W_S||^2
close to Q_S.  It is not: the ratio decays, so C_3 is essentially sum_S Q_S,
an undamped sum of Schur squares.

The ratio crosses below 1 at m = 16, so C_3 <= 4a C_2 is false in general.
The terms peak at r = 3 and then decay geometrically, but the number needed
grows with m: three nearly suffice at m = 14, four at m = 16, and at m = 20
five have not settled.  So the criterion is not a bounded-length statement.
