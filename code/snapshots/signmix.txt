  RESULT: the mixed column is zero everywhere, at every gap fraction. Children of a
  vertex always agree in sign, so cancellation never occurs and P29's premise was
  wrong -- there is no sign mixing to bound.
  That relocates the obstruction rather than removing it. With all children positive,
  F = lambda - sum 1/F_u is BELOW lambda automatically, and |F| >= lambda additionally
  needs the sum to exceed 2 lambda, i.e. roughly k >= 2 lambda B at a right-type
  vertex. The sign condition needs only k > lambda B. So |F| >= lambda is STRICTLY
  STRONGER than the sign-alternating requirement, not a weaker sign-free substitute,
  and it is the wrong replacement: the alternating certificate was the right invariant
  and its reach is exactly lambda^2 < Delta, which is binding_case.
