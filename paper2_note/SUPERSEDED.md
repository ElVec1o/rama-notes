# Superseded

This directory holds the single 68pp document that Papers 2a and 2b were split from.

- **Paper 2a** — `../paper2a_note/` — the closed form at first Betti number one, Hall's
  counterexample, the separation mechanism, and the minimum-degree threshold. 20pp.
- **Paper 2b** — `../paper2b_note/` — the biregular case: the weighted plane class, the complete
  bipartite margin and its decay, the coefficient ladder, the operator form, and the
  obstructions. 52pp.

The split is **editorial**. Nothing here is retracted by it: the two papers between them contain
this document's content, with Paper 2b restating without proof the nine statements and five
displays it takes from Paper 2a. The cut is at the point where the biregular material begins,
which is inside the section on Hall's counterexample rather than at the old part boundary.

This directory is kept for reference and for the DOI record. New work goes to `../paper2a_note/`
or `../paper2b_note/`.
